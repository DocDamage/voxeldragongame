# Game Design — Playable Rules and Progression

**Documentation release:** v0.1 · September 14, 2026  
**Status:** Detailed design proposal; not an implementation report  
**Basis:** H01; B03; A01; see [source register](./SOURCES_AND_EVIDENCE.md).  
**Rule:** Existing requirements remain binding. New numbers and detailed behavior are draft baselines for a bounded prototype, not claims of user approval or runtime validation.

## 1. The game the player is playing

WYRMFALL is a single-player action RPG about an outsider who learns to protect a real world made from the Origin Wyrm's dreamed civilizations. Explore finite generated regions, fight with responsive direct controls, obtain Diablo-style randomized equipment, change the land, and earn voluntary dragon allies. Horror encounters expand the created humanoid's powers. Zenith later expands travel with actual pilotable vehicles. [H01; B03]

The moment-to-moment loop is **choose a lead → travel/explore → fight or reshape a route → obtain loot/resources → improve a build or prepare → pursue a harder objective**. A regional climax yields a dragon whose actual companion and controlled play changes later adventures. Optional preparation must be useful without becoming compulsory maintenance.

Both cameras show the same game. Switching camera never changes the actor's identity, equipped items, damage formula, ability ownership, quest state, or difficulty. Click-to-move is a top-down option, not a replacement character or navigation-only game.

## 2. First playable content envelope

One created humanoid, two materially different usable loadouts, one compact Verdant Wilds region, Tidecross, Crowncut/underworks, Verdance's genuine boss and bond, compact/true dragon gameplay, a functional optional camp/activity chain, and the optional Counselor encounter form the connected slice. See [vertical slice](VERTICAL_SLICE.md).

One loadout is built first; the second is needed to demonstrate build variation before G5 closure. Full creator breadth, the remaining nine dragons, every rarity, and all later region content are preserved targets—not demanded by the first proof. Cars and the colony have their own later V1/Z1 milestones and cannot be counted as scenery substitutes.

## 3. Progression baseline

**Classless starting loadouts:** a melee starter and a ranged starter, with a magic-oriented starter later when real assets support it. Selecting an appearance or starting kit does not permanently lock a class. Weapon-specific abilities still require compatible equipment. There is no implied ability to swing a bow using sword animations.

The early balancing fixture covers levels 1–10. This is not the launch cap, campaign duration, or the final level range of Region 01. Level growth provides broad combat scaling; learned abilities, equipment and Echoes provide choices. Regional discoveries and dragon bonds are explicit flags, not additional mandatory currencies.

Draft XP needed from level `L` to `L+1` is `100 + 50*(L-1)` for this fixture. XP awards are saved as transactions, never generated by replaying a journal entry. Stop XP progression at the fixture cap and retain overflow only as diagnostic data; final cap/overflow behavior remains OPEN. Exact combat values belong only in [combat specification](systems/COMBAT_AND_NIGHTMARE_ECHOES.md).

The initial loadout has four active skill positions, basic attack, weapon secondary, dodge and quick consumable. Learned Echoes compete with ordinary skills in those same positions. Empty skill capacity is not a reason to create filler powers. Out-of-combat changes at a safe location are free in the slice; cooldowns follow ability IDs even when unequipped. Passive-tree breadth and final respec economy remain OPEN.

## 4. Loot and equipment

An authored item definition supplies a stable ID, compatible visual/attachment/Mutable mapping, family, allowed slots, affix pool, icon and localization key. Each acquired instance has a unique ID, definition ID, item level, rarity, exact rolled properties and upgrade state. Save rolled values; do not regenerate them from a seed after a balance update.

Initial rarity behavior is a BASELINE: Common has its base roll; Magic adds one eligible affix; Rare adds two distinct eligible affix families. A single model can represent many rolls. Epic/Legendary/Unique remain future catalogue possibilities, not deleted tiers. Do not count an item color as an implemented special effect.

Initial affix families are attack power, maximum health, armor, Focus efficiency and selected resistance. Rolls use curated item-level ranges and explicit exclusive families. Avoid impossible combinations or hidden level requirements. One later authored item modifier can prove an Echo interaction after its base ability works; it must not be required to use the earned power.

Proposed slots: main hand, off hand, head, torso, legs, gloves, feet and two accessory positions, enabled only with validated visuals. A two-handed item reserves both hands. Tool and fishing-rod attachments use existing equipment ownership and context, not free cloned inventory items. Unsupported slots must not appear as working equipment categories.

Inventory is a fixed-slot grid with stackable materials, sorting/filtering and stash access; no weight upkeep. Slot/stack counts are data parameters set after UI and owned inventory inspection. Quest facts, earned abilities and dragon bonds are outside the ordinary bag. Full inventory cannot erase a mandatory clue or prevent an Echo unlock. Ordinary loot stays in a persistent pickup/container until accepted; exact instance IDs survive re-entry and reload.

Equipping validates ownership and slot compatibility, prepares the visual result, commits slots and tracked GAS grants once, and removes old grants once. On failed appearance generation retain the last committed item/stats/appearance. Increasing maximum health through gear does not heal current health; lowering maximum clamps current health once. Repeated equip/unequip cannot generate health.

## 5. Reward policy

Normal encounters provide XP and a chance at useful equipment/materials; no guarantee of an upgrade each fight. Major quest rewards use named transaction IDs and an understandable purpose. Dragon bonding and designated horror resolution guarantee their permanent feature unlock on first valid completion; randomized equipment is a separate award.

One-time rewards remain one-time across callbacks, loading, dialogue repeat and actor respawn. When optional mercy/cure/trust resolution is authored, it grants the equivalent signature power with different narrative facts—not inferior progression. Hostile enemies do not all need redemption paths. A voluntary bond is never the same as purchasing a dragon or collecting its corpse.

Trading uses one slice currency, represented by the chosen inventory/economy owner. Initial vendor prices/refunds are curated data, not simulated city markets. No speculative auction house, casino-odds system, trade route economy or multiple region currencies. Starting equipment and the quarry storyline cannot depend on the player farming money.

## 6. Defeat, recovery and challenge

BASELINE difficulty uses authored regional/encounter bands, not constantly matching every enemy to player level. Normal enemies and elites teach readable patterns before bosses. Accessibility assists may alter telegraph duration, damage or input demands through a saved profile; do not silently change rewards or story outcomes for using them. Exact difficulty presets remain tuning work.

Humanoid defeat offers explicit retry at the current valid checkpoint. No item/XP deletion, permanent character loss or corpse-run requirement. Retry restores health/Focus as an explicit recovery action, ends transient beneficial/harmful effects, and retains committed world/quest/reward state. Cooldown policy is specified in the save contract and must not be reset through a routine load or camera switch. Unresolved boss attempts restart; already defeated/bonded dragons and resolved horror encounters do not.

Companion defeat is nonpermanent. The original dragon record becomes recovering, direct control ends safely, and a safe hub provides a clearly labeled recovery action. No feeding grind or replacement hatchling. Falling, drowning hazards, mounted defeat and invalid spawn recovery use named policies, not silent invulnerability.

Pause menus pause this single-player game. Backgrounding the application stops gameplay time by default. A document's use of asynchronous asset/save work does not make combat continue while the player thinks they paused.

## 7. Terrain and preparation

Dig and add terrain early. Deposits have finite saved yield; excavating ordinary ground is not an infinite ore source. Player-filled volume has a material budget and cannot regenerate mined deposits. Authored structural supports/protected envelopes use explicit localized rules; most approach terrain stays editable. See [editable-world rules](systems/EDITABLE_WORLD_RULES.md).

Fishing supplies real inventory catches. Cooking yields useful healing/buffs. Crafting converts real inputs to items. Camps support storage and useful work areas, with placement, snapping, removal and persistence. They do not introduce hunger, thirst, mandatory sleeping, crop chores, food spoilage, pet care or fuel upkeep. Main progress is playable while skipping these activities; development still must prove them.

## 8. Dragons and powers are different rewards

Dragon bonds grant a living independent ally, shared senses/direct control, compact companion form and supported riding. They do not grant ownership over the creature. Small form is functional in ordinary interiors; full form requires actual room. One active dragon initially, all bonds persistent.

Nightmare Echoes grant the humanoid usable permanent abilities. They are bounded player interpretations of demonstrated boss powers, not all bosses' full movesets at once. They do not automatically become dragon attacks. Real beast transformation restores the original created character afterward. See the dedicated system specifications for exact current proposals.

## 9. Progression and narrative boundary

The opening resolves the local quarry crisis and workers' fates; Meridess still rules the wider Reach. Hallowwood is the first concrete lead, not compulsory final region order. Full-world narrative contains all ten dragon arcs and the First Binder/Judge resolution. Launch scope is OPEN. No progression blocker should demand a collectible horror ability merely because it is interesting to use.

Selecting the home epilogue must preserve an explicit playable pre-departure save branch; choosing to stay continues the same world. No ending regenerates terrain, clears camps, removes inventory, dismisses all dragons or erases vehicles. The campaign capability map records future unlock relationships without producing nine extra regions during the first slice.
