# WP-22 — Jade Peaks production-region slice

**Dependencies:** WP-20 Jadefang rig proof; supplied `palace.zip`; working UE
5.8.2 host. **Scope:** JP-01 through JP-06 only.

## Objective

Create one bounded second production region that exercises the proven
multi-dragon, flight, combat, Echo, terrain, and unified-save architecture in a
new authored context. Jade Peaks is selected because Jadefang is the only later
dragon with a validated real rig and the supplied palace pack provides real
environment and character art.

## Acceptance

- **JP-01 Actual destination:** `L_JadePeaks` loads as a distinct playable map
  with finite GeoForge terrain, navigation, a safe humanoid arrival, and a
  return route to Region 01.
- **JP-02 Real supplied presentation:** the palace, guard/disciple, props, and
  Jadefang use imported supplied assets. Engine primitives may diagnose the
  fixture but cannot stand in for production characters or landmarks.
- **JP-03 Regional route:** arrival, palace approach, storm court, mirror well,
  and aerie form a coherent route with a humanoid path, compact-dragon
  clearance, and a true-form flight arena.
- **JP-04 Jadefang continuity:** the same saved Jadefang identity can enter,
  use compact/direct control, mount/flight, fight, and return without duplicate
  dragons or a second combat owner.
- **JP-05 Disciple and Mirror Step:** resolve the bounded temple-disciple
  encounter without compulsory killing; grant Mirror Step exactly once;
  validate a clear displacement and reject sealed, occupied, water/void, or
  over-400 cm destinations without spending Focus or cooldown.
- **JP-06 Unified persistence:** save/reload preserves Region 01, Jade Peaks
  facts/visits, both dragon identities, Echo unlock/cooldown, inventory, camp,
  terrain, and vehicle state without regeneration or duplicate rewards.

## Constraints

Mutable remains creator. GAS remains combat and ability authority.
`UWyrmSaveSubsystem` remains the sole persistence coordinator. Add one narrowly
scoped Jade Peaks fact owner; do not create parallel inventory, dragon, combat,
terrain, or save systems. Use supplied real art only. Do not expand into the
remaining Jade Peaks campaign, another dragon, another region, the colony, or
the WP-23 finale.

## Verification and stop condition

Compile `WYRMFALLEditor Win64 Development` against the installed UE 5.8.2
headers, run the source-declared native automation suite, execute a focused real
PIE proof for JP-01–06, and rerun relevant Region 01, WP-20 Jadefang, and WP-21
Moonbound regression proofs. Stop after those results are recorded, or at the
first precise missing real-art/engine blocker.
