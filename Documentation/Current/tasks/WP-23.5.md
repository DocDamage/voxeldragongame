takeoff, landing, and attack clips are present. This clears import feasibility
only; no `FWyrmDragonRigProfile` or PIE behavior has been accepted.

- Count Malvaine: `TVS_VoxelCathedral_Priest`.
- Hollow Twins: two separately authored instances based on
  `TVS_VoxelCathedral_Nun`.

  modular cemetery/church OBJ set.

331 separate static body-part meshes rather than ready-to-play skeletal
characters. Production therefore needs an authored modular assembly and
animation strategy plus collision, scale, material, and provenance clearance;
the asset is retained rather than discarded.

Production-facing identities use original WYRMFALL pun-names; source filenames
remain unchanged only for intake traceability. Current aliases are Ail-Yen,

completion leakage.

## Completed Second Turn Echo slice

The resolved Hollow Twins now manifest the optional permanent Second Turn
outcome after either encounter route. GAS activation spends 25 Focus and starts
a 14-second cooldown. The next eligible basic melee or ranged weapon strike
snapshots its pre-mitigation base damage; after 0.6 seconds a still-valid hostile
target receives one noncritical GAS repeat at 50% of that base. Generic,
secondary, reflected, and Echo-generated damage cannot consume or recurse it,
and the repeat cannot trigger Sanguine Strike or drain. Live PIE proves the
numerical contract and absence of completion leakage.

Proof: [Second Turn gameplay](../WP23_5_SECOND_TURN_SLICE_PROOF.md).
