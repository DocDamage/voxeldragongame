# WP-23.2 — Jade Peaks campaign closure

**Status:** COMPLETE — JC-01..08 passed in real PIE on September 19, 2026.
**Boundary:** one Jade Peaks closure slice only; no other WP-23 region.

## Objective

Close the verified WP-22 Jade Peaks baseline without changing JP-01..06
identities: present Emperor Wei Longzhu with supplied Palace King art, resolve
the emperor's exclusive seasonal claim on Jadefang through either diplomacy or
living defeat, grant the disciple's permanent Unseen Hand Echo exactly once,
and establish a safe Region 01 ↔ Jade Peaks travel/recovery contract.

- Mutable remains the creator; GAS remains combat and Echo authority.
- `UWyrmJadePeaksSubsystem` owns facts only.
- `AWyrmDragonCharacter` retains Jadefang identity, bond, Heartfold, riding,
  flight, combat, and direct-control ownership.
- `UWyrmWorldTravelSubsystem` is limited to two allowlisted routes and does not
  own save slots.
- `UWyrmSaveSubsystem` remains the sole persistence coordinator. Schema 5 adds
  region-keyed terrain/camp payloads and reads Schemas 1–4.
- Unseen Hand affects non-boss lighter enemies and explicitly authored movable
  props only. It cannot target bosses, terrain, buildings, or arbitrary physics.
- Optional Echo ownership is not a hidden tax on later world completion.
